# Home (`/`) — surface brief

Scope: storefront home page. Mode: **Persuade**. User-pinned direction (2026-11-15): remove the rainbow yarn ribbon and make the landing more striking, taking the supplied reference (`.impeccable/mocks/user-reference-ruled.png`, a product landing with a giant white background wordmark, a two-tone headline and an oversized hero object) as inspiration. The pinned reference beats any roll.

Audience and job: tourists looking for a handmade Peruvian piece; act = Ver catálogo / search / add to cart.
Constraints: Spanish only; logo used as given (its red is the brand colour); generated photos labelled "Imagen ilustrativa"; no invented claims; no folk overload or cheap-souvenir look. Kicker/eyebrow labels stay banned even though the reference has one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A studio shop window. A still life of real Peruvian crafts on white plinths, shot on the page's own grey, with the store's name enormous behind the pieces: the landing sells with products and figures from the first viewport. It refuses the photo banner over a card grid and decorative colour effects.

OWN-WORLD: cool grey page (#e9eaec) with a matching hero grey (#dfe1e6) that the header shares at the top; the "monatekeda" wordmark in white at 45%, stretched 1.6x, layered between the studio photo and a cut-out of its products; one brand red from the logo (bold heading words, nav, stat cards, primary pills, cart block); a muted red-grey for light heading lines; Geist light and bold, no italics; white cards with offset soft shadows; generated photos labelled "Imagen ilustrativa".

STORY: The visitor reads "Hecho a mano / en el Perú." over the still life, sees three recommended pieces and the store figures, scans the material index, and taps Explorar piezas, a pick or a material. Below: categories, a 3D top-products carousel, material tiles with live piece counts, the silver-restoration service, a filterable grid of available pieces, the red closing panel and the dark footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo, centred red nav with a categories mega-panel, white search block, red cart block, flush right). Background: the studio still life centred, the wordmark behind its products. Upper left: two-tone headline, tagline, Explorar piezas (red) and Ver categorías (dark). Lower left: "Te recomendamos" as a staircase of three translucent pick cards. Upper right: three red stat cards as a staircase (+10 años, +3000 productos, 24 talleres). Right: "Por material" index (5 items). Bottom right: social icons. Bottom centre: "Imagen ilustrativa".

FORM: Owner-pinned reference, iterated element by element with the owner (2026-11-15). Owner decisions are pinned: no italics, no blur on the photo, socials as icons only, cards without borders, wordmark behind the products and not faded.

FINISH: DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json updated 2026-11-15 to the shipped build; every shipping raster carries a provenance sidecar.
